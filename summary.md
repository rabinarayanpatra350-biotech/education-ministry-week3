# Executive Summary — Week 3: Project Work & Implementation

**Education Ministry Internship, Coimbatore** | Rabi Narayan Patra | 27 September 2026

## Project selected

NIPUN Bharat (national FLN mission) as implemented in Tamil Nadu through the Ennum Ezhuthum Mission — chosen via a 5-criteria, 4-candidate scored matrix (24/25), because learning outcomes is Tamil Nadu's weakest PGI 2.0 domain (41.2/100).

## Key numbers

- **Baseline**: NAS 2021 Class 3 national average — 62 language, 57 mathematics; ASER 2024 — 23.4% of Class 3 government-school children read a Class 2 text; 27.6% do 2-digit subtraction with borrowing (both all-time ASER highs)
- **Design**: NIPUN Grade 3 Lakshyas — read with meaning ~60 words per minute, numbers to 9,999, simple multiplication; universal FLN by 2026-27 (not later than Grade 5)
- **Funding**: over ₹6,000 crore in 3 years via Samagra Shiksha PAB (all India); Tamil Nadu: ₹152 crore (2022-23) + ₹110 crore (Classes 4-5 expansion)
- **Scale in TN**: 25.08 lakh students in 37,767 schools; 1.13 lakh teachers trained; 98% baseline assessment coverage; 65,000-teacher Telegram feedback channel; teacher activities cut 38% after feedback
- **Results**: TN Class 3 arithmetic +15 pp and Class 5 reading +10 pp between ASER 2022 and 2024; SLAS 2025 found TN Class 3 and 5 above NAS 2021 national averages; Rajya Sabha committee recommends extending the FLN mission to 2032
